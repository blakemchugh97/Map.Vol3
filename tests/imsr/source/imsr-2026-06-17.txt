Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Widespread elevated to critical conditions will persist across much of the West today. Across these areas, 
variable winds between 15-25 mph and relative humidity values between 5-15% are expected, with gusts up to 
35 mph across the Snake River Plain, northeastern Utah, southern Wyoming, and central Colorado. Breezy 
conditions will persist across the Northwest, but winds will be lower than yesterday. Temperatures nearing or 
exceeding 100 degrees are expected across the central California valleys and large po rtions of the Desert 
Southwest as well as the southern Great Plains and western Texas. Mixed wet and dry storms expected from 
northwest Arizona to southwest New Mexico today, with a chance for lightning across high elevations of the 
Sierra south of Tahoe later this afternoon into the evening. 
 
In the eastern U.S., scattered showers and thunderstorms will continue from the Dakotas east across the Great 
Lakes Region and portions of the Northeast, as well as from the Gulf Coast of Texas through parts of the 
Carolinas, with the heaviest precipitation is expected across the Great Lakes region. Hot and dry conditions will 
accompany isolated storm potential across Florida leading to elevated conditions. In Alaska, moderate conditions 
are expected with a chance for wet storms accompanied by lightning. Ov er central eastern Alaska, relative 
humidity values as low as 25% are expected, but winds will be light, less than 10 mph.
