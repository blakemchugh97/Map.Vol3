Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Critical fire weather conditions will revolve around scattered thunderstorms today across portions of southern 
Oregon, northern California, northern Nevada, and southern Idaho. These thunderstorms will be a mix of wet 
and dry, however considerable cloud- to-ground lightning is anticipated. The lightning threat will likely continue 
into the overnight hours. The other area of concern today will occur over the Great Basin due to the combination 
of gusty winds, dry relative humidity values and hot temperatures. Across the eastern 2/3rds of the county expect 
cooler temperatures with higher relative humidity values due to the passage of a cold front. The only area with 
moderate risk of significant fire potential will occur over southern Florida due to relatively dry conditions with the 
threat of thunderstorms. Over Alaska look for the warming and drying trend to continue w ith moderate risk of 
significant fires over eastern portions of the state.
