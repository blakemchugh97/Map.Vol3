Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
The southern Intermountain West will continue to see dry and very windy conditions today as the pattern across 
the West will be slow to change. With poor moisture recovery and another day of rapid drying, fire activity is likely 
to remain significant for the southern Great Basin, Southwest and Rocky Mountain geographic areas. Lightning 
holdovers may continue to emerge. Look for relative humidity from 8-15% across the region, with southwesterly 
winds gusting from 25-50 mph, locally higher in favored downslope terrain. Any extremely critical conditions will 
be more localized than on Saturday. The northern Intermountain West will be unseasonably chilly, with record 
cold high temperatures and some heavy snow in the higher elevations of Idaho, Montana and far nor thwestern 
Wyoming. Meanwhile, very hot, dry and breezy conditions will expand across the central and southern High 
Plains. Alaska will be cool and rainy, with minimal lightning and lower impacts to ongoing fires. Hot and drier 
conditions followed by isolated afternoon thunderstorms will bring increasing fire risks to the Florida peninsula 
and portions of the coastal Carolinas.
