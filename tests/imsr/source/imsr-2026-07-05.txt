Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Temperatures across the West will remain above normal, with minimum relative humidity 4- 20%, lowest in the 
central and eastern Great Basin to the central Rockies west of the Divide. Isolated mixed wet and dry 
thunderstorms will develop over northeast Calif ornia, southeast Oregon, and northwest Nevada, with a few 
ignitions likely. A few dry thunderstorms are possible overnight in the northern Great Basin. Isolated mixed wet 
and dry thunderstorms will also develop in the White Mountains of Arizona and Gila Mountains of New Mexico, 
with isolated wet thunderstorms southward to the Mexico border. Isolated mainly wet thunderstorms will develop 
along the Front Range into the central and southern High Plains. Hot temperatures will continue for one more 
day in the Carolinas, with isolated showers and thunderstorms developing in the afternoon. Scattered showers 
and thunderstorms will also develop from the northern Gulf Coast and Florida northward into the Lower Great 
Lakes then east into the Mid- Atlantic. Near normal t emperatures are expected in Alaska with isolated 
thunderstorms across the Interior, while breezy trade winds are expected in Hawai’i with isolated windward 
showers.
