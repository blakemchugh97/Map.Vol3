from the Upper Midwest to the Mid -Atlantic. Behind the front, strong northerly winds will develop across 
Minnesota and the Upper Great Lakes. Meanwhile, a tropical depression over the Gulf of America will continue 
to supply deep moisture and energy for heavy rainfall along the eastern Gulf Coast and southern Atlantic states. 
Strong winds are also expected across South Florida and the Carolina coast, where limited rainfall may contribute 
to elevated fire potential.
