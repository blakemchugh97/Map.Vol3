Rockies and northwestern Great Basin today from westerly winds 15-25 mph with gusts 25-35 mph amid relative 
humidity of 5-20%. These breezy and dry conditions will spread into the central Great Basin later today, as well. 
Hot temperatures near 100°F will continue in much of the Great Basin, central Rockies, and northern Rockies

developing overnight. Isolated to scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop in the Great Lakes to the 
Southeast, with scattered to numerous wet thunderstorms in Florida likely to temporarily ease conditions there.
