Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
The highest confidence for isolated dry thunderstorms today covers north central and eastern Idaho, extending 
into southern Montana and western Wyoming. These storms will become wetter as they move eastward. A lower 
probability, but potentially high-impact, area for isolated dry thunderstorms extends from eastern Washington to 
northwest Montana. A break in the monsoon begins today as drier air moves into central and western Arizona, 
shifting the thunderstorm focus into New Mexico. Even drier conditions will  expand across the Southwest by 
tomorrow. Meanwhile, isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will continue for Colorado and the Front 
Range. Very low RH of 3-15% is expanding across the Mojave Desert and much of the Great Basin, with poor 
overnight recovery of 10 -30% in many locations. Unseasonably low RH of 8 -20% will also develop in the 
Southwest, West Slope, Inland Northwest, and northern Rockies. Temperatures will be 5- 15°F above normal 
across many of these areas, particularly the Southwest, though outside of thunderstorm activity winds will remain 
relatively light except for the Cascade gaps. Widespread heavy thunderstorm rains are possible across parts of 
the Dakotas and the Missouri River Valley, spreading eastward tonight. Lingering showers and a few 
thunderstorms will continue across New England, while locally heavy rainfall focuses along and south of a 
stationary frontal boundary from central Florida to the Lower Mississippi Valley. South Florida is expected to
