Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dangerously hot temperatures will expand across the southern half of California, the southern Great Basin, and 
the Southwest, with highs at lower elevations ranging from 105°F to 120°F, and locally up to 125°F. 
Temperatures near or above 100°F will spread northward through northern California, the northern Great Basin, 
the West Slope, and Montana, east of the Divide. In the Northwest, maximum temperatures will climb to 5 to 15 
degrees above normal, generally in the 85 -95°F range. Well -above-normal temperatures will persist in the 
southern Plains, while the Upper Midwest, western Great Lakes, and Northeast experience near - to below-
normal temperatures. 
 
Very dry conditions will accompany the heat, with minimum RH between 3% and 15% for much of inland 
California, the Great Basin, southwest Wyoming, and the West Slope. The Inland Northwest and Northern 
Rockies will see RH drop to 10- 20%, though winds will generally remain light. An uptick in mainly wet 
thunderstorms is expected from the Front Range into the eastern half of New Mexico, while pronounced drying 
takes place across much of Arizona. Elsewhere, thunderstorms are likely for the Midwest, the Ozark Plateau, 
and the Lower Mississippi River Valley. 
 
A major wind event will develop across the Inland Northwest on Saturday, with strong westerly winds impacting 
the Northern Rockies and portions of the northern Great Basin Saturday night and Sunday. This unseasonably 
strong weather system will result in wi despread gusts of 35-50 mph with localized gusts exceeding 50 mph for 
the high terrain. Widespread critical to locally extreme conditions can be expected.
