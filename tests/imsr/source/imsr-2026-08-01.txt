Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Widespread critical to extreme conditions will develop across the Inland Northwest by late morning. These 
conditions are expected to worsen into the afternoon and evening while spreading eastward into the Northern 
Rockies and the far northern Great Basin. Unseasonably strong wind gusts of 35– 50 mph are anticipated in 
many areas, with minimum RH 5-25%. Localized gusts of 60+ mph are likely over portions of northwest Montana 
tonight. Under these extreme conditions, many existing fires will display extreme growth, and new ignitions will 
be extremely resistant to control. Ahead of the strong cold front, a very large expanse of record or near -record 
heat will affect areas from inland California and the Southwest to the Great Basin and Northern Rockies, with 
some locations approaching monthly records for August. RH of 3– 15% will return across inland California, the 
Great Basin, southwest Wyoming, and the West Slope. Moderate southwest winds may create elevated 
conditions in these areas for several hours. Pronounced drying is also expected in northern Arizona and the Four 
Corners, with minimum RH falling to 7–18%. Very isolated dry thunderstorms are possible for southwest Montana 
and north central Idaho into the Rockies with wet thunderstorms for central and eastern New Mexico. Cooler -
than-average temperatures east of the Mississippi River Valley with areas of strong to severe thunderstorms 
from the Ohio River Valley to the Southeast.
