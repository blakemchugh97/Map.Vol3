Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
8/2/2026 Critical conditions will persist across the northern Rockies, northern Great Basin, and northern Plains 
through this evening as a strong cold front shifts east and south. Westerly winds near and east of the Divide in 
Montana will reach 20-30 mph with gusts up to 50 mph amid 10- 20% RH. Near and ahead of the front across 
most of the Great Basin to the central Rockies, expect west to southwest winds of 15- 25 mph, gusts of 25-40 
mph, and 4-12% RH. Despite cooler-than-normal temperatures, elevated conditions will also linger in the Inland 
Northwest, particularly southeast Oregon. Temperatures remain well above normal ahead of the front across the 
southern half of  the West, Wyoming, and northern Plains, with highs near 100°F in the northern Plains and 
extreme temperatures of 100-120°F in the lower elevations of California, the central/southern Great Basin, and 
Southwest. Very low RH is expected across California, the Great Basin, northern Arizona, the Four Corners, and 
the West Slope. Southeast Arizona and southern New Mexico will see isolated to scattered wet thunderstorms. 
Meanwhile, areas east of the Mississippi River Valley, including the Southeast and most of Florida, will 
experience near to slightly below normal temperatures with widespread showers and thunderstorms.
