Zealand are assigned to support large fires in the Northwest Area.

been deployed to Klamath Falls, OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National 
Guard) and respective support personnel have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire 
operations nationally.

Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Modrite fire. An 
Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Continued dry and breezy with locally elevated risks in northeast California, southern Idaho and Wyoming. Mixed 
wet/dry thunderstorms will be isolated to scattered, similar to Monday, from the eastern Sierra to the West Slope 
but becoming wetter farther east. Arizona will see an uptick in wet thunderstorms, while low-end scattered mixed 
wet/dry thunderstorms persist in New Mexico. Temperatures will be near normal for both states, and across the 
southern Great Basin and West Slope. Above -normal temperatures continue in California, though the recent 
widespread, extreme heat is slowly ending. Widespread elevated conditions will develop in the central and 
southern Plains due to breezy southerly winds (gusts near 30 mph), dry conditions, and extreme heat. Maximum 
temperatures will reach 105°F–110°F from Nebraska to north Texas, with isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms 
expected across the High Plains. The Southeast will see temperatures rise above normal as the ridge aloft 
strengthens, while strong drying and very  isolated thunderstorms continue along the Gulf Coast and Florida. 
Alaska’s eastern Interior remains warm with isolated thunderstorm activity. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin 
Islands will have a marked increase in shower activity, likely the most active day of the week.
