One 15-member incident management team and 59 fireline management personnel from Australia and New 
Zealand are assigned to support large fires in the Northwest Area. 
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) have 
been deployed to Klamath Falls, OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National 
Guard) and respective support personnel have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire 
operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Modrite fire. An 
Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Elevated to locally critical fire conditions are expected across eastern New Mexico, Texas, Oklahoma, and 
southern Kansas due to breezy south winds gusting up to 35 mph, very hot temperatures near 105°F and RH of 
10-25%.  In the West, most areas will see l ight winds outside of thunderstorms and temperatures up to about 
five degrees above normal. RH will drop to 10- 25% across much of the West, and 20- 30% in the Greater Four 
Corners. Lightning with little or no wetting rain is the primary concern f or central/northern California, north 
central/northeast Washington, the Idaho Panhandle, and southwestern/south -central Montana. Elsewhere, 
isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are forecast from the northern Cascades into northwest 
Montana, and from eastern Idaho into Wyoming. A backdoor cold front will bring higher RH and scattered wetter 
thunderstorms east of the Divide in Montana with isolated to scattered mostly wet thunderstorms from the eastern 
Sierra to the West Slope, and into the South west. Scattered thunderstorms are expected from the Mid-
Mississippi Valley to the Carolina coast and Florida. Alaska’s eastern Interior remains warm and mostly dry with 
very isolated thunderstorms. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands will be warmer and drier with strengthening 
trade winds.
