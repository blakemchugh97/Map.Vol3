Understanding the IMSR 
 
IMSR Map 
 
One 15-member incident management team and 59 fireline management personnel from Australia and New 
Zealand are assigned to support large fires in the Northwest Area. 
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) have 
been deployed to Klamath Falls, OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National 
Guard) and respective support personnel have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire 
operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Modrite fire. An 
Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent. 
 
On August 11, a contract firefighter from Alaska experienced a fatal medical emergency while assigned to the 
Bear Trap incident in Minnesota. The firefighting community expresses its condolences to the family and 
friends of the deceased.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Isolated to scattered thunderstorms producing little to no wetting rainfall are expected to impact more of the 
Northwest, central and northeast California, northwest Nevada, Idaho, and far western Montana as compared to 
Wednesday. This will include parts of the Olympic Peninsula in addition to areas in/near the Cascades and the 
Sierra Front. Farther south and east, scattered thunderstorms will be wetter and generally less efficient at new 
ignitions in the central/southern Great Basin, the eastern two-thirds of Montana, much of Wyoming and Colorado, 
as well as the Southwest. RH overall in the West will continue to moderate, with minima at 15 -35% for most 
areas and temperatures near to slightly below normal. Southerly winds gusting up to 40 mph combined with very 
hot and dry conditions (RH 10-25%) will create widespread elevated to critical conditions in the southern Plains. 
Isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are also possible across the High Plains. Very hot, dry, and breezy 
conditions are expected from the ArkLaTex into the Ozark Plateau. Alaska’s eastern Interior remains warm and 
mostly dry with isolated thunderstorms. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands will be very warm and dry with 
high-risk significant fire potential due to stronger trade winds.
