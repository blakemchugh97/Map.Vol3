Three 15-member incident management teams and 196 fireline management personnel from Australia and 
New Zealand are assigned to support large fires in the Northwest and Great Basin geographic areas.  
 
One Large Airtanker and one Birddog from Manitoba, Canada have been deployed to Boise, ID in support of 
wildland fire operations. 
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) have 
been deployed to Klamath Falls, OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National 
Guard) and respective support personnel have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire 
operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Modrite fire. An 
Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Expect another  very hot  day across most of the Southern Plains today  due to a strong ridge of high 
pressure. High temperatures in the upper 90s to middle 100s are expected from the eastern half of Texas east 
to Mississippi. These hot temperatures will combine with relative humidity values ranging from 25 -35% across 
northern Texas and Oklahoma and wind gusts from 20 -25 mph resulting in enhanced fire weather 
conditions.  On the periphery of this strong ridge thunderstorms will form  from the Desert Southwest northward 
along the Rockies as well as along the Mid-Atlantic Seaboard and into Florida.  These storms will be primarily 
wet. Another cluster of thunderstorms will develop over the northern Rockies and over extreme northern 
Washington due to a weak upper-level disturbance. These storms will be a mix of wet and dry.  The final area of 
concern will be Hawaii due to the presence of Tropical Storm Lala. Although the storm will be pushing west of 
the Islands tonight, wind gusts of 50-60 mph with torrential flooding rains are still expected.
