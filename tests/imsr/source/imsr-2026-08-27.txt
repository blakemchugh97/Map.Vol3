Two 15-member incident management teams and 212 fireline personnel from Australia and New Zealand are 
assigned to support large fires in the Northwest and Great Basin geographic areas.  
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) and one from the 152nd 
Airlift Wing (NV Air National Guard), with respective support personnel, have been deployed to Klamath Falls, 
OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National Guard) 
have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Little Giant fire. 
An Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Breezy west-southwest winds with gusts of 35– 45 mph and RH of 5– 20% will create elevated fire conditions 
across the Inland Northwest, northern Rockies, and northern Great Basin. Locally critical conditions are possible 
from northeast California and northwest Nevada into southeast Oregon and southwest Idaho. Scattered mixed 
wet/dry thunderstorms are expected across western Montana, central/eastern Idaho, and the central Rockies, 
with drier storms possible in northeast Washington and north Idaho. Isolated mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will 
also extend from the eastern Sierra through the Great Basin to the West Slope, while wetter storms remain more 
numerous in Colorado and N ew Mexico east of the Divide, and the western High Plains. Thunderstorms are 
expected from south Texas to the Southeast ahead of a slow -moving cold front, with thunderstorm activity 
increasing into the Mid- Atlantic and portions of the Northeast. Dry, seasonable weather will prevail across the 
Midwest and Great Lakes.
