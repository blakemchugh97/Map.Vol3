Two 15-member incident management teams and 212 fireline personnel from Australia and New Zealand are 
assigned to support large fires in the Northwest and Great Basin geographic areas.  
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) and one from the 152nd 
Airlift Wing (NV Air National Guard), with respective support personnel, have been deployed to Klamath Falls, 
OR. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National Guard) 
have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Little Giant fire. 
An Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are expected across portions of central/southern California, 
Nevada, Utah, Idaho, western Montana and Wyoming. Central and northern California will have increased risk 
of isolated dry lightning, elevating potential, while thunderstorms will be more numerous and wetter near the 
Sierra Crest. Low RH of 10–25% and gusty winds are expected across the Great Basin and into Wyoming and 
the West Slope, with wind gusts up to 45 mph possible in southern and western  Wyoming.  In the Southwest, 
isolated to scattered mixed wet/dry thunderstorms will favor Arizona and eastern/southern New Mexico. Across 
the eastern U.S., conditions will remain generally dry and seasonable for most areas, with scattered 
thunderstorms pos sible along the East Coast and across portions of the Southeast. Isolated to scattered 
thunderstorms are also expected across southern Texas and the Lower Mississippi Valley. Tropical Storm Dolly 
is expected to weaken while approaching the northeastern Car ibbean, but associated moisture may increase 
showers and thunderstorms across Puerto Rico, but impacts remain uncertain.
