Incident Management Situation Report 
Monday August 31, 2026 – 0730 MDT 
National Preparedness Level 5 
 
 
National Fire Activity: 
 
 
 
Understanding the IMSR 
 
IMSR Map 
 
Two 15-member incident management teams and 212 fireline personnel from Australia and New Zealand are 
assigned to support large fires in the Northwest and Great Basin geographic areas.  
 
Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers from the 302nd Airlift Wing (Air Force Reserve, CO) and one from the 152nd 
Airlift Wing (NV Air National Guard), with respective support personnel, have been deployed to Santa Maria, 
CA. Two MAFFS C-130 airtankers and support personnel from the 146th Airlift Wing (CA Air National Guard) 
have been deployed to Redding, CA to support wildland fire operations nationally. 
 
Two hundred forty soldiers from the 1st Battalion, 23rd Infantry Regiment, 7th Infantry Division based out of 
Joint Base Lewis McChord (JBLM) are deployed in support of wildland fire operations on the Little Giant fire. 
An Incident Management Organization (IMO) is assigned to support the military contingent. 
 
On August 30, a federal wildlife officer with the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service was fatally injured while assisting 
with evacuations on the Railroad South Fire in Oklahoma. The firefighting community extends its condolences 
to the family and friends of the deceased.

Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Breezy southerly winds persist across much of the central and southern Plains, especially central/western 
Oklahoma and the Texas Panhandle where gusts up to 35 mph will create elevated to locally critical conditions 
amid very hot temperatures and low RH. Locally breezy winds and low RH generally 10- 20% will also create 
elevated conditions across parts of the northern Great Basin into northeast California and southeastern Oregon. 
Strong westerly winds into the northern Plains with occasional gusts exceeding 40 mph across parts of eastern 
Montana into North Dakota. More robust monsoon moisture plume into Arizona and western New Mexico 
extending into much of the northern and eastern Great Basin and greater Four Corners and West Slope, resulting 
in scattered to numerous mixed wet/dry thunderstorms. Isolated to scatted mixed wet/dry thunderstorms east of 
the Divide in Colorado, southern Wyoming extending into the central Plains. Isolated to scattered thunderstorms 
develop over parts of central and south Texas, becoming more numerous along the Gulf Coast, Florida and 
southeast Atlantic coast.
