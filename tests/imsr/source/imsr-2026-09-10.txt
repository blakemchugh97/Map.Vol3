Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dry and breezy west winds will continue across Wyoming and spread into southeast Montana today as winds 
gust to 45 mph amid minimum relative humidity of 7-20% creating elevated to locally critical conditions. Breezy 
winds are also expected for the Inland Northwest into the northern Great Basin, but relative humidity is expected 
to be higher and mostly above 15% if not 20%. Stronger winds may develop in north-central Montana, but relative 
humidity will be elevated with scattered showers in the evening to mit igate. Hot temperatures will continue in 
California today along with low relative humidity of 10 -20% but winds will be light. A cold front will slowly move 
southward into the Texas Panhandle and much of Oklahoma today, with scattered showers and thunderstorms 
bringing areas of relief, but new ignitions are possible, as well. Scattered showers and thunderstorms will also 
develop from the Mid -Mississippi Valley to the Mid -Atlantic, while drier conditions are expected in the Great 
Lakes and Northeast.
