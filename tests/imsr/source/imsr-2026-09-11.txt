Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dry and breezy westerly winds continue across parts of Wyoming, with wind gusts to 40-50mph amid minimum 
relative humidity of 10-20%, creating elevated to locally critical conditions. Breezy winds are also expected for 
much of the Great Basin and Four Corners, extending into the Central Rockies amid RH lowering to 5- 20%, 
bringing moderate significant fire potential to some parts of the area. Well above normal temperatures will 
continue in the Plains, extending into the Midwest, with temperatures near 100 °F and moderate significant fire 
potential expected across parts of the southern Plains. A cold front will slowly move southward into the Texas 
Panhandle and much of Oklahoma today, with scattered showers and thunderstorms bringing areas of relief, but 
new ignitions are possible, as well. Scattered showers and thunderstorms will extend from the Mid- Mississippi 
Valley to the Mid-Atlantic, as well as the Southeast, while mostly dry conditions are expected in the Great Lakes 
and Northeast.
