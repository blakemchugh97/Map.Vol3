Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dry and breezy west to southwesterly winds will bring moderate significant fire potential to parts of the central 
Great Basin and the central Rockies, with wind gusts of 30-40mph amid minimum of RH 8-20%. However, locally 
stronger wind gusts of 40- 50mph will bring high-risk significant fire potential to parts of Wyoming. Through the 
plains, breezy south to southeasterly winds with gusts of 25-40mph will develop, bringing moderate to high-risk 
potential to parts of the northern High Plains, as well as much o f Texas. Across the southern Plains, southerly 
winds with gusts of 25-40mph are expected amid temperatures near 100 °F and minimum RH to 20-35%. Across 
the northern Plains, temperatures are expected to be below usual with RH mostly above 35%. Mixed wet/dry  
thunderstorms are possible across the southern Plains as well as the Four Corners area. Elsewhere, the 
significant fire potential is little to low, with below usual temperatures about the west coast as well as the northern 
Rockies and remainder of the nor thern Plains. Scattered showers are expected across northern Idaho and 
northern Montana, extending into the Mid- West and Mid- Mississippi Valley. Showers and thunderstorms also 
expected Southeast, Northeast, and Mid-Atlantic coast.
