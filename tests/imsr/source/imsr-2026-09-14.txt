Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dry and breezy west to northwesterly winds will bring moderate significant fire potential to parts of the northern 
and central Rockies and adjacent High Plains, with wind gusts of 40 -50mph amid minimum of RH 8 -25%. 
Stronger gusts are expected across the hi gh peaks. Locally dry and breezy northwesterly winds will bring 
moderate potential to parts of the north California coast and Sacramento Valley, with gusts of 20 -30mph amid 
RH of 10 -20%. Through the southern and central Plains into the Mississippi Valley, much above usual 
temperatures continue with temperatures reaching near 100 °F, bringing moderate significant fire potential to 
much of the southern Plains and lower Mississippi Valley. However, across the western  half of Texas, locally 
breezy southerly winds with gusts of 25 -35mph amid RH of 20 -30% will bring high-risk conditions to the area. 
Through the north Plains into the Mid -west, cool but breezy winds are expected with widespread gusts of 40 -
50mph. Across th e remainder of the country, significant fire potential is little to low with mostly below usual 
temperatures across much of the north and west of the country. Mixed wet/dry thunderstorms are possible across 
the southern Plains as well as the Four Corners area. Thunderstorms with widespread rainfall is expected across 
the central Plains, extending into the Mid-West and Great Lakes area
