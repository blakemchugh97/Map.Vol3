Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Hot temperatures will continue across the southern Plains today with highs near 100°F, with locally breezy 
southerly winds 12-20 mph and higher gusts amid minimum relative humidity of 20-30%. Scattered showers and 
thunderstorms will develop in the Southwest and along a cold front from southern Colorado east northeastward 
into the Great Lakes. Behind the front, winds are expected to be mostly lighter than previous days across the 
Great Basin and Wyoming, but minimum relative humidity will remain low and 8-20%. Low relative humidity and 
light winds are also expected across much of California, as well. An upper -level low will drop into Washington 
tonight with isolated light showers, and temperatures will remain below normal in the Northwest into the northern 
Rockies. Isolated showers and thunderstorms will continue across Florida as well as the Lower Mississippi Valley 
and southeast Texas.
