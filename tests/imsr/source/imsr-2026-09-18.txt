and widespread above normal temperatures will spread east into the southern Appalachians. Minimum relative 
humidity is expected to be 20-35% from the southern Plains to the Lower Mississippi Valley, with breezy south

northern Rockies, while abundant cloud cover continues in the Greater Four Corners with isolated afternoon 
showers and thunderstorms. Cool temperatures will continue in California while temperatures remain slightly 
above normal in Washington.
