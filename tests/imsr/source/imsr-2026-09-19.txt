Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Temperatures will remain hot across the southern plains and middle Mississippi Valley today with highs reaching 
near 100°F. Breezy winds with gusts 25 to 35 mph will develop from the Texas panhandle across central 
Oklahoma this afternoon. Flood Watches remain in effect for northern Wyoming and southcentral Montana today 
as heavy rain continues beneath the upper level trough as it moves east. The eastward movement of the trough 
will also cause breezy winds gusting to 30 mph to develop along the Front Range a nd in the eastern Sierra.  A 
warming and drying trend will begin across the Pacific Northwest as a weak and transient ridge of high pressure 
builds over the area. Well below normal temperatures continue across the Great Basin and Northern Rockies 
while California remains near to slightly below normal. Relative humidity across northern California will drop to 
10-20% while RHs drop to 7-15% across Nevada and the Mojave Desert.
