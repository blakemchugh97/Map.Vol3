Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Hot temperatures, very dry fuels, and RH dropping to 20-30% persists across the Lower Mississippi Valley today. 
The eastward progression of the upper trough and slight weakening of the ridge over the southeast will allow 
wet thunderstorms to develop this afternoon over central Oklahoma. This will bring relief to the relative humidity 
there but bring gusty winds as well. Eastern Oklahoma and Texas and the Lower Mississippi Valley will remain 
dry. Wet thunderstorms develop again today over eastern Montana, Wy oming, and the High Plains with many 
areas likely to see near 1” of rain. Light winds persist across the Pacific Northwest, though warming and drying 
will allow relative humidity to drop to 15 -25% this afternoon across the Columbia Basin. Relative humidity  will 
drop to 7-18% across Nevada, the Mojave Desert, and the Central Valley this afternoon but winds will remain 
light with gusts less than 25 mph. Wind gusts to 25 to 30 mph are likely again today across the Front Range and 
the eastern Sierra.
