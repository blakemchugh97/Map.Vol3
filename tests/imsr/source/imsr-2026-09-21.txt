Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
The ridge of high pressure over the southeast will continue to weaken, but temperatures will still rise into the mid 
to upper 90s this afternoon from the lower Mississippi Valley north through Tennessee. Relative humidity will 
bottom out near 25-30% again today as well. In the Pacific Northwest, a trough aloft will bring breezy west winds 
gusting to 25 mph to cascade gaps and relative humidity will fall to 15- 30% again this afternoon east of the 
Cascades. Further south, slightly above average temperatures a re expected across northern California today 
along with breezy overnight winds and poor relative humidity recovery after RH falls to 10- 20% this afternoon. 
Isolated mixed wet and dry thunderstorms will develop this afternoon across southern and western Nev ada 
including across the Sierra Front. Isolated thunderstorms will also develop across the lower Mississippi Valley 
and across west Texas today, bring gusty outflow winds with them. Conditions trend drier across the desert 
southwest, though high temperatur es remain mostly below normal. Today will be the driest day of the week 
across the southern Front Range with relative humidity dropping into the teens.
