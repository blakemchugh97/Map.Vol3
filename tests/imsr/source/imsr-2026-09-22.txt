Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
The lower Mississippi Valley will be the main area of concern today as warm temperatures and dry relative 
humidities re-establish after isolated thunderstorms moved over the area yesterday. Relative humidity will fall to 
35-40% as temperatures rise back into the low to mid 90s, and there will be a few isolated thunderstorms today 
as well. The reorientation of the high pressure ridge over the southeast will allow southerly flow to draw another 
round of monsoonal moisture into New Mexico and eastern Arizona, and 2-5 inches of rain is expected there 
today. Breezy west winds will develop through Cascade gaps this afternoon with gusts to 35 mph, slightly 
stronger than yesterday. This will include the Columbia Gorge. Relative humidity will drop to 10 -20% across 
northern California with breezy winds and poor overnight humidity recovery again today, though significant fire 
potential will remain low. A weak cold front moving south through the southern plains will continue to bring 
isolated showers and thunderstorms to parts of Texas and western Oklahoma.
