Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Above normal temperatures will continue on the southern Plains to the Lower Mississippi Valley today but 
temperatures will not be as extreme as the past few weeks. Some locally low relative humidity near 30% is also 
expected in portions of northeast Texas into the Lower Mississippi Valley. Temperatures will also be a bit above 
normal in the northwestern U.S. with seasonally low relative humidity and light winds. Deep tropical moisture will 
continue to affect eastern Arizona into New Mexico and southern Colorado today with widespread showers and 
scattered thunderstorms, with areas of flooding likely near and east of the New Mexico central mountains. 
Scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop across the northern Plains today as a weak cold front moves 
through. Widespread showers will continue in the Mid-Atlantic today, but rainfall will not be as heavy as the past 
two days. Developing low pressure off the coast will also bring strong northeast winds to much of the East Coast 
from southern New England to the Carolinas.
