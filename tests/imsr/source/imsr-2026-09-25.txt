Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Southwesterly winds 15-25 mph with gusts 30-40 mph will develop across northeast California into the northern 
Great Basin and southwest to central Montana today. Minimum relative humidity will be lowest from 10-20% from 
northern Nevada into central Montana with areas of elevated conditions expected. Breezy west winds will also 
occur in the Columbia Basin but will be accompanied by higher humidity above 30% and isolated to scattered 
showers. More widespread rainfall is expected near and west of the Cascades in Oregon and Washington, with 
wetting rain also expected in northern Idaho to western Montana. Warm and dry conditions are expected in 
southern California, but winds will be light outside the deserts. Widespread showers and scattered thunderstorms 
will continue in New Mexico, but areas of flooding are likely to be more localized than previous days. Above 
normal temperatures will continue in the southern Plains to Lower Mississippi Valley but with light winds and 
minimum relative humidity near or above 30%.  A strong Nor’easter will develop off the Mid -Atlantic coast with 
strong northeast winds expected near the coast, with rain expected to spread into southern New England 
overnight. Areas of heavy rain are expected for the Big Island of Hawai’i and Maui as Hurricane Nolo approaches.
