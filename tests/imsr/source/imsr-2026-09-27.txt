Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Above normal temperatures will persist across the southern Plains into the Lower Mississippi Valley today, with 
areas of low afternoon relative humidity as low as 25% but winds will be light. In the West, temperatures will 
average below normal as winds are  mostly light, with areas of low afternoon relative humidity in central and 
southern California into Nevada. Scattered showers and isolated thunderstorms will develop later in the day 
across the Southwest as moisture from Hurricane Polo off the Mexico coast moves northward. Isolated showers 
and thunderstorms will also develop in the Lower Mississippi Valley, while rain occurs along a cold front in the 
northern Plains. Heavy rain is expected in much of southern and eastern New England today as the strong 
Nor’easter continues to affect the areas, with strong northeast winds and coastal flooding continuing, as well. 
Winds will slowly weaken in Hawai’i as Hurricane Nolo moves westward away from the islands, with a decrease 
in showers also expected.
