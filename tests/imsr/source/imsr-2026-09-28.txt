Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Above normal temperatures will persist across the southern Plains into the Lower Mississippi Valley today, but 
light southerly flow off the Gulf will bring additional low level moisture with minimum relative humidity forecast to 
remain above 35%. In addition, isolated showers and thunderstorms in the afternoon and evening will produce 
localized wetting rain. Drier conditions with minimum relative humidity of 25-35% will persist in the Southeast. In 
the West, abundant moisture from Hurricane Polo will move into the Southwest with widespread showers, 
scattered thunderstorms, and localized flooding. Scattered showers and thunderstorms are also likely to develop 
farther to the north and east into the central Rockies and much of the central and northern Plains. Farther west, 
dry conditions are expected for California, the western Great Basin, Northwest, and northern Rockies, amid 
below normal temperatures. Rain and wind will diminish i n the Northeast today, with drier conditions and 
moderate trade winds in Hawai’i.
