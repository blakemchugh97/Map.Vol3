Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Locally breezy northerly winds in portions of the Sacramento Valley and southern California diminish this 
morning, but relative humidity of 10-15% will persist amid above normal temperatures. Lighter winds are 
expected today across the northwestern U.S. as a weak cold front moves through with temperatures either 
slightly above or below normal ahead of or behind the front, respectively. An upper-level low will move through 
the Southwest and Colorado with scattered showers and thunderstorms and below normal temperatures. A 
cold front will stretch from Colorado into the Great Lakes with widespread showers and scattered 
thunderstorms. Widespread showers and thunderstorms, with a few severe thunderstorms, will produce areas 
of heavy rain and flooding in central Texas and Oklahoma today as moisture from the remnants of Hurricane 
Polo move across the region. Farther east, breezy south winds of 10-18 mph are expected amid above normal 
temperatures and minimum relative humidity of 25-35% creating elevated conditions. Otherwise, isolated 
showers and thunderstorms will continue across the Gulf Coast and Florida.
