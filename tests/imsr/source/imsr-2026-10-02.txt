Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A cold front will slowly move across east Texas, the Tennessee Valley, Appalachians, Mid -Atlantic, and 
Northeast today bringing scattered to widespread showers and thunderstorms. The heaviest rain will fall in east 
Texas and the Ohio to Tennessee Valleys. Isolated to scattered showers and thunderstorms will also develop in 
much of the Southeast ahead of the front, with the most widespread storms along the northern Gulf Coast and 
in Florida. In the West, the warming trend continues as temperatures rise to 5 -15°F above normal across most 
of the West, with only portions of the Southwest remaining near to below normal. Minimum relative humidity will 
remain very low in California and Nevada at 5-15% with areas of poor overnight recovery, with higher minima of 
15-30% in the northern Intermountain West. Winds will be light in California and Nevada, but locally breezy west 
winds will develop near and east of the Divide in Montana and Wyoming.
