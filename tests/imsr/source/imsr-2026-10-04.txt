Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Very warm and dry conditions will expand across more of the West today with afternoon highs 10 -20°F above 
normal from the northern Rockies and Northwest south to California and Nevada. Maxima near 100°F are 
expected in the California Central Valley and portions of southern California. Very low relative humidity of 5-15% 
will persist in California and Nevada, spreading into Oregon and Idaho, and poor overnight recovery of 20-40% 
is expected for mid-slopes and ridges. However, temperatures will remain near to below normal in the Southwest. 
A stalled cold front along the northern Gulf Coast and into the Southeast will bring scattered to widespread 
showers and thunderstorms and areas of heavy rain bringing significant relief from the very dry conditions of 
late.
