Predictive Services Discussion:

normal from the northern Rockies and Northwest south to California and Nevada. Maxima near 100°F are 
expected in the California Central Valley and portions of southern California. Very low relative humidity of 5-20% 
will persist in California, Nevada, eastern Oregon and Idaho, and poor overnight recovery of 20-40% is expected 
for mid-slopes and ridges. Breezy westerly winds are expe cted near and east of the Divide in Montana and 
Wyoming creating areas of elevated conditions as minimum relative humidity falls to 10 -20%. However, 
temperatures will remain near to below normal in the Southwest. A stalled cold front along the northern Gul f 
Coast will keep scattered to widespread showers and thunderstorms with areas of heavy rain.
