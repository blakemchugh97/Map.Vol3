Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Very hot temperatures reaching into the low 100s will continue across the California Central Valley and portions 
of southern California this afternoon with very low relative humidity of 10-20%. Winds will be light across most of 
California however. Afternoon highs 5 -15°F above normal continue across the Great Basin and into Montana 
and Wyoming. Winds in Montana and Wyoming will be locally breezy, but generally not as windy as Monday. 
Near normal temperatures are expected across the Southwes t and through the lower Mississippi Valley, while 
below normal temperatures are expected across the east coast. Moderate to heavy rain will continue across 
Florida and southern Georgia. Scattered showers and isolated thunderstorms are expected across the Wasatch 
and the Colorado Rockies. Mainly dry conditions will prevail elsewhere.
