Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A major pattern change will develop today as a trough aloft drops south across the West with a strong leading 
cold front. Winds ahead of the front may gust as high as 50 mph across the high terrain and lee slopes from 
southern California to Montana. Temperatures will still be hot and relative humidity still dry and a wide swath of 
near critical to critical fire weather is expected for Montana and Wyoming and near the Sierra Front. Hot and dry 
conditions with east winds gusting 15 to 35 mph will bring a crit ical burn environment to the lower Mississippi 
Valley out ahead of Hurricane Isaias. By late Friday night, Isaias is anticipated to make landfall bringing heavy 
rain, strong winds, and storm surge to the Gulf Coast.  Cooler temperatures, moderated relative  humidity, and 
light rain are expected across Washington state and western Oregon.
