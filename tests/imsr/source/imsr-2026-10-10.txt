Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A deep trough of low pressure will continue to drop south over the western US, bringing a significant cooldown 
to the Pacific Northwest and the Great Basin. Winds will still be strong along the Sierra Front with gusts 40 -45 
mph and along the leading edge of the trough east of the Rockies, but precipitation will begin to increase to the 
west. Snow is likely across western Montana and Wyoming. Showers and isolated thunderstorms will move 
across southern California in the afternoon, where southerly and westerl y winds of 20-30 mph with gusts to 50 
mph will continue across mountain ridges and desert passes. Down south, Hurricane Isaias is rapidly weakening 
as it moves inland across Alabama, though a narrow corridor of critical RH below 35% and north and northwest 
winds gusting 25-45 mph is likely to develop west of the storm in the afternoon.
