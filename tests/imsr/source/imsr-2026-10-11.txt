Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
The pattern change continues to progress today with widespread moisture and cool conditions across the Rocky 
Mountains and the Great Basin. Northern California will remain gusty and dry however with RH dropping into the 
teens, though some rainfall is expec ted across the northeast and far eastside. Moisture from the remnants of 
Hurricane Rachel will be drawn northward to bring areas of moderate to heavy rain to southern California, 
Arizona, and southern Nevada. Winds will also be quite strong with gusts to 5 0 mph across southern California 
and Arizona. High temperatures will be 10 to 20 degrees below normal across most areas west of the Rockies. 
The Pacific Northwest will also see some breezy north and northeast winds with gusts to 35 mph across the 
Columbia Basin and most of Oregon. Dry and hot weather will follow Isaias across the south today, but winds 
will be much lighter.
