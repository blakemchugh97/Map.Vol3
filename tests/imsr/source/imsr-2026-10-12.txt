Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A ridge of high pressure building in the wake of Isaias will bring hot, unstable, and windy conditions to northern 
Louisiana and east Arkansas today. A trough of low pressure over the inland Northwest will weaken today, 
though below normal temperatures will continue there and across the Great Basin with widespread precipitation 
across the Great Basin as well. Temperatures begin to rebound over northern California, but there will be less 
wind today and still some showers over the Far Eastside and Tahoe regio ns. Showers and isolated 
thunderstorms continue across southern California along with south to west winds of 20 to 30 mph with gusts to 
50 mph across the mountain ridges and desert passes. Temperatures there will cool to 15 to 20 degrees below 
normal. Cool  conditions continue across the northern Rockies with widespread precipitation across the 
Southwest.
