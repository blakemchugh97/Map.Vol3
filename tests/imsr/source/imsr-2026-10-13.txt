Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Warm and dry conditions continue across eastern Arkansas, northern Louisiana, and Mississippi today. Northern 
California will see a return to above normal temperatures but with lighter winds while southern California sees 
slight warming but temperatures staying 10 to 15 degrees below normal. Below normal temperatures and 
widespread precipitation continue across the Great Basin. This is likely being a season ending event for areas 
of the Great Basin still seeing fire activity. Cool conditions continue across the northern Rock ies. Across the 
Rockies and the Southwest, precipitation shifts to east of the Continental Divide with minimal fire potential 
through this week. The Northwest will see a return to near normal temperatures as high pressure over the 
northeast Pacific drifts over the area.
