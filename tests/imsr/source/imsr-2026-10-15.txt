Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Strong easterly winds are expected across California and the Southwest, with the greatest concern in California. 
Moderate Santa Ana winds are expected in southern California, with strong downslope winds for the west slope 
of the Sierra creating elevated conditions for these areas. However, rainfall last week from Tropical Storm Rachel 
will mitigate conditions across far southern California. A slow-moving cold front will bring scattered to widespread 
showers and thunderstorms from west Texas to the Mid -Mississippi Valley, with widespread rain expected in 
much of the Midwest, as well. Dry conditions will persist in the Southeast, with isolated thunderstorms in south 
Florida. The northern half of the West will be dry with a warming trend as temperatures rise to 10 -20°F above 
normal. A strong storm in the Gulf of Alaska will bring heavy rain to the south coast and panhandle of Alaska, 
with areas of flooding possible in the southern panhandle.
