Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Strong, dry easterly winds from the west slopes of the Sierra to southern California will diminish today, but dry 
conditions will persist with above normal temperatures. Lighter winds are also expected in the Southwest today. 
Breezy northerly winds will co ntinue in the Lower Mississippi Valley with minimum relative humidity as low as 
25% creating elevated conditions. Farther north, breezy and dry southwest winds will develop in the Upper 
Midwest, but recent rainfall will mitigate the conditions except in portions of western Minnesota. Breezy and dry 
northwesterly winds will develop in northern New England, as well, but the rainfall earlier this week will mitigate 
the conditions in drought-stricken coastal Maine. Much of the rest of the country will be dry, as well, with scattered 
showers and thunderstorms limited to eastern North Carolina and south Florida, isolated showers in Arizona, 
and light rain in western Washington. A cold front will move through the Alaska Interior today with light snowfall 
and mixed precipitation, while drier weather returns to south -central Alaska and showers continue in the 
panhandle. Breezy and moist trade winds will continue in Hawai’i with occasional showers.
