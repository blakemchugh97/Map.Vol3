Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A Pacific storm will continue to move through the Northwest and northern Rockies with light to moderate rain the 
lower elevations and snow above 5,000 to 6,000 feet. Dry air with near -normal temperatures are expected in 
California and the Greater Four Corn ers. Rain will diminish in the Upper Midwest during the day, but 
temperatures will remain above normal. Temperatures will also remain above normal across the Mid and Lower 
Mississippi Valley, with below average minimum relative humidity of 18 -35%. However, winds will continue to 
remain light in the region.  A weakening dry cold front will move through the Mid -Atlantic, with breezy and dry 
northwest winds in New England diminishing during the day. Another strong storm will move into western Alaska 
with areas of heavy snow and strong winds, especially for the Seward Peninsula. Deep tropical moisture will 
move across Hawai’i today and tonight with widespread showers and scattered thunderstorms, focused on the 
western islands.
