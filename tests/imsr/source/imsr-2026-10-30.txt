Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
A dry cold front will continue to move through the Ohio Valley and Mid -Atlantic today, with breezy northwest 
winds in the Northeast. These winds will be accompanied by relative humidity above 40% keeping fire potential 
low. Dry and warm conditions will con tinue in the Southeast and Lower Mississippi Valley, but relative humidity 
will be higher than previous days and generally above 30%, except in portions of the southern Appalachians 
which may drop as low as 25%. Scattered showers and thunderstorms will develop on the central and southern 
Plains due to moisture from the remnants of Pacific Tropical Storm Vance. Farther west, moisture from Vance 
will also move into the Southwest and northward into the Great Basin and central Rockies, where scattered 
showers and isolated thunderstorms are expected. Due to the warm nature of the system, snow will be confined 
to the highest elevations of the central Rockies. Precipitation will wind down today in western Alaska while strong 
winds occur off the Alaska Range in port ions of the central and eastern Interior, while heavy snow is expected 
in the Alaska Range and Chugach Mountains. Trade winds return to Hawai’i but elevated low-level moisture will 
result in scattered and locally heavy windward showers.
