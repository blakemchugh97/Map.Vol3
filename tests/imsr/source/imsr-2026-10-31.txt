Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Very dry air is expected in the central and southern Appalachians with minimum relative humidity falling to 20 -
30%, with local areas in the teens. However, winds are expected to be light today. Farther north, locally breezy 
northwest winds will develop in New England amid minimum relative humidity as low as 30% creating locally 
elevated conditions along the Maine coast. Above normal temperatures will continue in the Mississippi Valley, 
but Gulf moisture will move northward with widespread showers and scatte red thunderstorms in the Lower to 
Mid-Mississippi Valley bringing some relief from the recent very dry conditions. Farther north into the Great 
Lakes, precipitation will be much more isolated and light. In the West, deep tropical moisture will move northward 
through southern California and Arizona into the Great Basin with widespread showers and isolated 
thunderstorms, with scattered and light showers for northern California and the Northwest. Strong southerly 
winds will begin to wind down today across the Alaska Range, while scattered light precipitation occurs in western 
and south-central Alaska. Trade winds will weaken across Hawai’i amid a moist airmass but showers will be less 
than previous days and mostly occur on the windward sides.
