Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Breezy and dry northwest winds will continue across the Southeast today, with locally elevated conditions in 
coastal Georgia and the eastern Carolinas as minimum relative humidity falls to 25-35%. Dry conditions will also 
persist to the west in the Lower Mississippi Valley, but winds will be lighter than the past two days. Moist 
northwest flow will continue across the Great Lakes and Northeast with scattered rain and snow showers 
downwind of the lakes and across northern New York and New England. Dry conditions will continue in the 
northern Rockies to the northern Plains amid above normal temperatures. A weak upper-level low will move 
through the Southwest into the southern Plains with scattered showers and thunderstorms, most numerous in 
the southern Plains, with snow in the higher elevations of the Southwest. A brief break is expected along the 
West Coast before the next Pacific storm moves into the Northwest overnight, with rain and high-elevation snow 
near and west of the Cascades. Mostly dry and seasonably cold temperatures are expected in Alaska, while 
strong trade winds develop in Hawai’i with light windward showers.
