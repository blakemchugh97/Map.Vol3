Predictive Services Discussion:  
 
Dry conditions will persist east of the Mississippi River to the southeast Atlantic coast with minimum relative 
humidity 25-40% but winds will be light. Moist northwest flow will continue across the Great Lakes and Northeast 
with scattered rain and snow showers downwind of the lakes in New York and in New England. Dry conditions 
will continue in the northern Rockies to the northern Plains amid above normal temperatures, while light showers 
are expected along the northwestern Gulf Coast. The next Pacific storm continues to slowly move into West, 
with most precipitation along the coast and near/west of the Cascades and Sierra, falling as snow above 4,000 
to 7,000 feet. The associated cold front will move east of the mountains overnight and bring precipitation to the 
western Great Basin and eventually the northern Rockies. Mostly dry and seasonably cold temperatures are 
expected in Alaska aside from a few flurries, while breezy trade winds persist in Hawai’i with light windward 
showers.
