showers and isolated thunderstorms, with most rainfall amounts light. Mostly dry conditions are expected in the 
Southwest and Plains, while a weakening cold front brings scattered showers and thunderstorms to California 
and the northern half of the West, with snow levels from 5,000 feet in the Sierra to 2,000 feet near the Canadian 
border. Mostly dry conditions will occur in Alaska except for light showers in southwest Alaska with colder 
temperatures. In Hawai’i, breezy to strong trade winds will continue with more stable conditions resulting in fewer 
windward showers.
